About Cyber I.T Masters

Cyber I.T Masters is the managed IT and technology retail division of Mbulaheni Group — a premier black-owned holding company headquartered in Polokwane, South Africa, established in 2015. We supply certified tech products and deliver professional IT solutions to businesses, government entities, and households across South Africa.

Our Story
Mbulaheni Group is a diversified investment powerhouse spanning five core service sectors: civil engineering and construction, information technology services, fuel supply, commercial cleaning, and security services. The group combines international standards with deep local expertise to deliver quality solutions across the African continent.

Cyber I.T Masters is the technology arm — focused on managed IT services, tech product retail, network infrastructure, cybersecurity, and 24/7 technical support. We are committed to transforming communities through job creation, skills development, and sustainable business practices.
