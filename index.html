Common Questions

MSP Services — FAQ

What exactly is a Managed Service Provider (MSP)?
An MSP takes responsibility for managing and monitoring your IT infrastructure on an ongoing basis for a fixed monthly fee. Instead of calling IT only when something breaks, you have a team proactively keeping your systems healthy, secure and running 24/7. Think of us as your outsourced IT department.
How much does managed IT cost for a small business?
Our Essentials plan starts at R1,499/month for up to 5 devices. For most small businesses, the Professional plan (R3,999/month for up to 20 devices) delivers the best value. We also offer custom pricing for larger environments. All plans exclude VAT.
Do we need to replace our existing hardware to sign up?
Not necessarily. We do a free IT assessment first to understand your environment. If hardware replacements are needed, we'll flag them with pricing options — we supply hardware through our tech shop at competitive rates. We never force unnecessary upgrades.
What does "24/7 monitoring" actually mean in practice?
We deploy lightweight monitoring agents on your network devices and endpoints. These continuously report status, performance metrics and security events to our platform. When something anomalous happens — a device going offline, a suspicious login, high CPU — we're alerted immediately and act before it becomes a problem for you.
How is cybersecurity handled under the MSP model?
From the Professional plan upwards, we deploy EDR, next-gen antivirus, email phishing protection and firewall management. We run monthly vulnerability scans and push security patches. On the Business plan and above, you get SIEM log monitoring and a formal incident response procedure.
Do you help with POPIA compliance?
Yes. Our Business and Enterprise plans include POPIA gap assessments, security policy documentation, breach response procedures and staff awareness training. POPIA carries significant penalties for non-compliance — we make sure your IT environment meets the requirements.
Can I buy hardware from you as well as take an MSP plan?
Absolutely — that's the whole idea. We are both an MSP and a tech hardware retailer. MSP clients get priority pricing on hardware from our store. Browse 276+ products at shop.mbulahenigroup.co.za — networking gear, computers, CCTV, gaming peripherals and more. Delivered nationwide.
Do you serve businesses outside of Limpopo / Johannesburg?
Yes — we serve businesses across South Africa. Remote monitoring and management works regardless of location. For on-site visits, we cover Limpopo and Gauteng on all plans, with on-site visits to other provinces available at travel cost for Business and Enterprise clients.
